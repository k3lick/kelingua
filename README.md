# Kelingua

Translate what you type, paste or speak — 240 languages, with auto-detect.

Kelingua is a translator app for Android. Paste a WhatsApp chat or a social
media post, type a message, or speak, and get it in another language —
including Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Kanuri and Tiv.

- **Translate:** type or paste, auto-detect, up to about 4,000 words in one
  paste. Long texts translate in parts, with progress shown and Cancel
  keeping the part already translated.
- **Conversation:** two people, two languages, hands-free on speakerphone —
  listen, translate, speak back, repeat.
- **Learn:** practise a language with word and sentence drills, scored on
  content, word order and spelling — never on accent.
- **Saved:** your saved translations and history stay on your device only.
  No account, no sign-in, no ads.

## Install

Download **Kelingua-standalone-v3.0.4.apk** from the
[latest release](https://github.com/malwar77/kelingua/releases/latest)
and install it on your Android phone (Android 8.0+). It installs over
earlier Kelingua versions without uninstalling, and your Saved items are
kept.

## Privacy

Translations are sent to third-party translation services (MyMemory and
Google) so they can be translated. Saved items, history and Learn progress
stay only on your device. There is no account and nothing is uploaded to
Kelingua's servers by this app.

Full policy: https://kelickhub.com.ng/privacy

## The Pro version

Kelingua Pro is a separate web version with accounts, Free/Pro plans and
