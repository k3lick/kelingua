## Full description (paste as the app description)

Kelingua turns what you type, paste or speak into another language —
240 languages, with auto-detect.

Paste a WhatsApp chat or a social media post and get it translated.
Type a message and hear it spoken. Or sit two people down, pick two
languages, and let Kelingua carry the conversation hands-free on
speakerphone — it listens, translates, and speaks back.

240 languages including Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Kanuri and Tiv.
Long texts up to about 4,000 words translate in parts, with progress
shown. Learn mode helps you practise with word and sentence drills.
Saved translations and history stay on your device only — no account,
no sign-in, no ads.

## Permissions to declare in review notes

- INTERNET + ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — translation uses the MyMemory and
  Google translation services.
- RECORD_AUDIO — voice input and hands-free Conversation mode only.
  No audio is recorded or stored by Kelingua; speech is recognised on
  the device or by Android's speech service.
- No location, contacts, SMS, phone, storage or background-service
  permission is used.

## Data safety (what the app does with user data)

- Translations are sent to MyMemory and Google to be translated.
- Saved items, history and Learn progress stay only on the device.
- No account, no analytics, no ads, no data sold or shared with anyone
  except the two translation services above.
- No data is collected by Kelingua itself.

## Seller account — BLOCKER, read before you start

Samsung does not let an individual publish with just a Samsung account.
To distribute free or paid apps you need Commercial Seller status, and
outside mainland China that means a Corporate Commercial Seller request
with D-U-N-S certification — D-U-N-S verification alone can take up to
10 business days. There is no sign-up or annual fee, but you need:

1. A Samsung account, then a Seller Portal account.
2. A registered business with a D-U-N-S Number.
3. A bank account or PayPal for financial information.

Kel is self-employed with no registered business on record, so this step
cannot be completed as-is. Options: register a business and get a D-U-N-S
number first, or publish through a registered company. Do not start the
submission until the seller status is approved — the app upload comes
after it. Also note: since 2 September 2026 Samsung blocks Seller Portal
submissions whose binary is not Android-developer-verified (ADV), so the
signing certificate may first need registering under Google Play Console
→ Android developer verification for this package name.
