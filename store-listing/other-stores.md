## Full description (all stores)

Kelingua translates what you type, paste or speak — 240 languages,
including Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Kanuri and Tiv. Auto-detects the language
you type in, reads translations aloud, and saves them on your phone.

- Translate: type or paste, auto-detect, listen, copy, save. Works with
  long text up to about 4,000 words at a time.
- Conversation: two people pick their languages and talk, hands-free.
- Learn: practise with a score that checks your words and word order.
- Saved: your translations and history, kept only on your device.
- No account, no ads, no sign-in.

Kelingua uses free translation engines on the internet, so it needs a
connection to translate. Voice works where your phone supports the
language.

## 1. Huawei AppGallery — BEST NEXT STORE

Why first: individuals CAN publish (Samsung is the only store that
demands a company). Registration is free; identity verification takes
1–2 working days; app review usually 1–3 working days.

- Console: AppGallery Connect (developer.huawei.com) — needs a HUAWEI ID.
- Individual verification: a bank document showing your full name in
  English (valid at least 6 months) + ID card, passport or driver's
  licence. No D-U-N-S needed for an individual. Warning: an individual
  account can never become an enterprise account later, and the country
  you pick during verification cannot be changed — pick carefully.
- At app creation you must enter the SHA-256 certificate fingerprint:
  a9d7bf8a1a859f2e661ef9b1f060ffd522e06b47ad464afdad2ad26852772a5a
- Upload the APK (AppGallery accepts APK). Category: Education.
- Compatibility check: Kelingua does not depend on Google Play services
  (translation is over the internet, voice is the phone's own
  speech recogniser), so it should run on Huawei phones without Google
  services. State this in the listing notes if asked.
- Kel's steps: create a HUAWEI ID → register as a developer → complete
  identity verification → create the app in AppGallery Connect → upload
  APK + icon + screenshots + description → submit.

## 2. Uptodown — EASIEST, NO IDENTITY DOCUMENTS UP FRONT

- Console: https://en.uptodown.com/developers-console — free. Sign up
  with a Google account or the form (organisation name, email,
  password only). There is a developer identity-verification step
  before the first submission goes live.
- Submit: Apps → Add new app → upload the signed APK (package name is
  filled in automatically from the APK) → icon (square PNG, minimum
  256x256), name, website, short + full description → Submit for review.
- Every file is scanned across 70+ VirusTotal engines. Rejection
  reasons include low-quality images and apps with no clear function —
  Kelingua is fine on both. A 1024x500 featured banner is recommended
  but not mandatory.
- Category: Education (confirm against Uptodown's own list in the console).
- Kel's steps: create the account → complete verification → add the app
  → upload APK + assets → submit.
